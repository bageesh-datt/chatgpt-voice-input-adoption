# ChatGPT Voice Input Adoption – India

### End-to-End Product Management Case Study

**Product:** ChatGPT Mobile  
**Market:** India  
**Project Focus:** Voice Input Adoption  
**Role:** Product Management  
**Author:** Bageesh Datt Mishra

---

## Project Overview

This case study explores how to increase Voice Input adoption among ChatGPT mobile users in India.

The project follows a three-milestone product management journey, covering market research, user segmentation, problem validation, solution prioritisation, MVP definition, UX design, product metrics and growth strategy.

## Project Objectives

- Understand India's voice-tech and mobile usage landscape.
- Identify why users prefer typing over ChatGPT Voice Input.
- Conduct user research and define the core problem.
- Explore and prioritise potential product solutions.
- Define an MVP and measure its potential impact.

## Milestone 1 – Systems Thinking & Mapping Outcomes

**Focus:** Understanding the market landscape, business goals and product outcomes.

Key work:
- Analysed India's voice-tech and mobile usage landscape.
- Evaluated ChatGPT's existing Voice Input experience.
- Benchmarked Google Assistant, Gemini and WhatsApp.
- Identified discoverability, onboarding and habit-formation gaps.
- Mapped business outcomes to product outcomes and KPIs.

## Milestone 2 – User Research & Problem Framing

**Focus:** Understanding why users are not using ChatGPT Voice Input.

Key work:
- Identified five user segments.
- Selected students as the primary target segment.
- Conducted a survey with 38 respondents.
- Interviewed five users.
- Identified barriers related to transcription accuracy, control, accent recognition and public usage.
- Developed a Problem Framing Canvas.

## Milestone 3 – Prioritisation, Metrics & Growth

**Focus:** Prioritising solutions and defining the proposed product experience.

Three solution directions explored:
1. Smart Voice Suggestions
2. Adaptive Voice Mode
3. AI Voice Draft

**Selected MVP: AI Voice Draft – Review, Enhance & Send**

Key features:
- Editable Transcript
- AI-Enhanced Draft
- Draft Comparison
- Review & Confirm
- Re-edit / Switch Draft
- Continue Conversation

Additional deliverables:
- Solution prioritisation
- User flow
- Annotated wireframes
- Product success metrics
- A/B experimentation plan
- AI-assisted prototype

## Final Product Recommendation

AI Voice Draft aims to combine the speed of voice input with the control of typing by allowing users to review, edit and confirm their prompts before submitting them.
